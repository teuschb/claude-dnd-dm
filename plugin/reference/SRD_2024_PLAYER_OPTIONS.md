    - **Potent Cantrip:** A creature that avoids your damaging cantrip (miss or successful save) still takes half damage, with no other cantrip effect.
  - **Level 6**: Sculpt Spells
    - **Sculpt Spells:** When casting an Evocation spell affecting other visible creatures, choose a number of them equal to 1 + the spell’s level — they auto-succeed their save and take no damage on a would-be-half-damage success.
  - **Level 10**: Empowered Evocation
    - **Empowered Evocation:** Add your Intelligence modifier to one damage roll of an Evocation spell you cast.
  - **Level 14**: Overchannel
    - **Overchannel:** Casting a damage-dealing spell (slot level 1-5) can deal maximum damage instead of rolling. First use each Long Rest: no cost. Subsequent uses before a Long Rest: take 2d12 Necrotic damage per spell slot level (ignores Resistance/Immunity), increasing by 1d12 per additional use.
