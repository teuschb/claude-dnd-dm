# <Campaign Name> — Player Feedback Ledger

Durable record of what the table has asked for and what changed, so future
sessions steer from it instead of rediscovering it.
**Append at every debrief / whenever a player gives feedback.** Plain file — safe
to edit while the campaign is loaded.

## Check-in ritual (every ~2 sessions, and at each milestone level-up)
Ask the table (via the table owner, or `party_push` to the shared feed) three
questions, one at a time:
1. Best moment so far? (what to do more of)
2. Anything that dragged or felt off? (what to cut)
3. Which thread do you want to see again — a PC hook, an NPC, an open quest?

Record answers below under a dated row; then act on them within one session.
