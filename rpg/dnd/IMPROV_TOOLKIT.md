# Improvisation toolkit

Roll with `d20-mcp` (`roll expression=1d10`), never pick by mood and never invent an outcome you didn't roll. Use a result as a seed: reskin it to fit the established fiction, and if it contradicts it, reroll. Log whatever you create in the NPC/location record or `game_state.notes`. Never tell players a result came off a table.

| 1 | Time: something closes, leaves or spoils |
