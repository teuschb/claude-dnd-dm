# SRD 2024 Magic Items — Reference

Condensed from a manually verified transcription (aidedd.org). Most entries are
summarized to their core mechanics rather than verbatim (legendary/artifact items
especially trim flavor text and long random-effect tables) — good enough to hand out
as loot and look up on the fly; check the source material for full legendary text
if one becomes a major campaign item.

### Adamantine Armor
*Armor (Any Medium or Heavy, Except Hide Armor), Uncommon*
This suit of armor is reinforced with adamantine, one of the hardest substances in existence. While you're wearing it, any Critical Hit against you becomes a normal hit.

### Adamantine Weapon
*Weapon (Any Ammunition or Melee Weapon), Uncommon*
This weapon or piece of ammunition is made of adamantine, one of the hardest substances in existence. Whenever this weapon or piece of ammunition hits an object, the hit is a Critical Hit.

### Alchemy Jug
*Wondrous Item, Uncommon*
This ceramic jug appears to be able to hold a gallon of liquid and weighs 12 pounds whether full or empty. You can take a Magic action and name one liquid from a table to cause the jug to produce the chosen liquid (water, wine, acid, oil, beer, etc, up to a max amount per liquid), and pour it out via a Utilize action, up to 2 gallons per minute. Once the jug starts producing a liquid, it can't produce a different one until the next dawn.

### Ammunition, +1, +2, or +3
*Weapon (Any Ammunition), Uncommon (+1), Rare (+2), or Very Rare (+3)*
You have a bonus to attack rolls and damage rolls made with this piece of magic ammunition, determined by rarity. Once it hits a target, the ammunition is no longer magical.

### Ammunition of Slaying
*Weapon (Any Ammunition), Very Rare*
This magic ammunition is meant to slay creatures of a particular type. If a creature of that type takes damage from it, it makes a DC 17 Constitution saving throw, taking an extra 6d10 Force damage on a failed save or half as much on a success. After dealing its extra damage, the ammunition becomes nonmagical.

### Amulet of Health
*Wondrous Item, Rare (Requires Attunement)*
Your Constitution is 19 while you wear this amulet. It has no effect on you if your Constitution is 19 or higher without it.

### Amulet of Proof against Detection and Location
*Wondrous Item, Uncommon (Requires Attunement)*
While wearing this amulet, you can't be targeted by Divination spells or perceived through magical scrying sensors unless you allow it.

### Amulet of the Planes
*Wondrous Item, Very Rare (Requires Attunement)*
While wearing this amulet, you can take a Magic action to name a location on another plane of existence and attempt an Intelligence (Arcana) check to cast Plane Shift there; on a failure, you and nearby creatures travel to a random destination instead.

### Animated Shield
*Armor (Shield), Very Rare (Requires Attunement)*
While holding this Shield, you can take a Bonus Action to cause it to animate and hover in your space, protecting you as if wielded, leaving your hands free, for 1 minute or until dismissed.

### Apparatus of Kwalish
*Wondrous Item, Legendary*
A sealed iron barrel that transforms into a giant lobster-like submersible vehicle piloted from within, with claws, lights, and various lever-controlled functions.

### Armor, +1, +2, or +3
*Armor (Any Light, Medium, or Heavy), Rare (+1), Very Rare (+2), or Legendary (+3)*
You have a bonus to Armor Class while wearing this armor, determined by its rarity.

### Armor of Gleaming
*Armor (Any Light, Medium, or Heavy), Common*
This armor never gets dirty.

### Armor of Invulnerability
*Armor (Plate Armor), Legendary (Requires Attunement)*
You have Resistance to Bludgeoning, Piercing, and Slashing damage while wearing this armor, and can take a Magic action to gain Immunity to those damage types for 10 minutes once per dawn.

### Armor of Resistance
*Armor (Any Light, Medium, or Heavy), Rare (Requires Attunement)*
You have Resistance to one type of damage (DM's choice or random) while you wear this armor.

### Armor of Vulnerability
*Armor (Any Light, Medium, or Heavy), Rare (Requires Attunement)*
While wearing this armor, you have Resistance to one physical damage type. Curse: attuning curses you with Vulnerability to the other two physical damage types until targeted by Remove Curse.

### Arrow-Catching Shield

### Axe of the Dwarvish Lords
*Weapon (Battleaxe), Artifact (Requires Attunement)*

### Bag of Beans
